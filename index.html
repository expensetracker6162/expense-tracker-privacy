Privacy Policy

1.Scope of this policy

This policy explains how the Expense Tracker Android application (package name com.ammrwahab.expensetracker, referred to here as "the app", "we", "us" or "our") handles information. It applies to the app and to this policy page. It does not apply to other apps, websites or services you may reach from your device.

2.Summary

The short version, before the details:

  • You can use the app without creating an account or giving us any personal details.
  • The expenses you enter are saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • We operate no servers for the app and receive no information from it.
  • There is no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking code in the app.
  • You can delete your data at any time from inside the app or by uninstalling it.

3.Information stored on your device

When you add an expense, the app saves these details locally:

  • the amount you enter;
  • the category you choose (Food, Transport, Shopping, Bills or Other);
  • an optional note, if you type one;
  • the date of the expense;
  • a randomly generated identifier used internally to identify that entry.

This data is written to the app's own private storage area on your device. It is not a user account and it is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or any device identifier. Because it never leaves your device, we cannot see it, read it or recover it for you.

The app does not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, calendar, health data, financial account details, or advertising identifiers.

4.Information we collect

We collect no personal information and no usage data. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking technology. We do not create user profiles, and we do not receive statistics about how the app is used.

This policy page is a static website. It does not use cookies, and it does not load fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers.

5.Network access

The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission and contains no networking code. It cannot transmit your expenses or any other information off your device, and it works entirely offline.

The app contains a link to this privacy policy. Tapping that link asks your device to open the page in your usual web browser. That request is made by the browser, not by the app, and is subject to the browser provider's own privacy policy. The app itself does not send any data with that request.

6.Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information, because we never receive it. There are no third-party service providers, advertising partners or data brokers involved in the app.

The only situation in which information could be disclosed is if you choose to share it yourself — for example, by showing your screen to someone or by backing up your device. Those actions are under your control.

7.App permissions

The app declares no Android runtime permissions. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, photos, SMS or phone state, and it does not request network access.

If a future version of the app ever needs a new permission, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described under Changes to this policy.

8.Device backups

Android includes an optional system backup service. If you have enabled backup on your device, the operating system — not the app — may include app data in the backup of your device that is stored in your own Google account. That backup is controlled by your device settings and by Google's terms and privacy policy.

You can turn this off or remove existing backups in your device's system settings (typically Settings → System → Backup). We have no access to those backups.

9.Data retention and deletion

Your expenses are kept on your device for as long as you keep them. There is no copy on any server, so there is no remote data for us to retain or delete.

Delete individual entries

On the main screen, tap an expense (or its delete icon) and confirm. The entry is removed from your device immediately.

Delete everything

  • Uninstall the app — this removes the app and its stored expenses from your device.
  • Clear the app's storage — go to Settings → Apps → Expense Tracker → Storage and choose Clear storage (wording may vary by device).

Neither action requires a request to us, and neither can be undone by us. If you had device backup enabled, an older copy may remain in your Google account until it expires or you delete it; see Device backups.

10.Security

Your expenses are stored in the app's private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps on a normal, unmodified device. Because the app does not transmit data, there is no server-side database that could be breached, and no data of yours is stored by us.

No method of storage is completely secure. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, or to a rooted or compromised device, may be able to read app data. Keeping your device locked, up to date and protected by a screen lock is the most effective way to protect the entries you record.

11.Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose expense log and is not directed to children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will help you address it.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or legal requirements change. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will describe it in the app's release notes on Google Play as well.

Continued use of the app after an updated policy takes effect means you accept the updated policy. The current version is always available at this page.
